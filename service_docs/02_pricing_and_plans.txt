Nimbus Stack — Pricing & Plans FAQ

What plans does Nimbus Stack offer?
We offer three plans: Starter ($8/user/month), Growth ($15/user/month), and Enterprise (custom pricing). All plans are billed monthly or annually, with a 15% discount for annual billing.

What's included in the Starter plan?
Starter includes up to 50 workspace members, Kanban and List boards, basic time tracking, and Slack integration. It does not include Timeline view or GitHub sync.

What's included in the Growth plan?
Growth includes everything in Starter, plus unlimited members, Timeline view, GitHub sync, advanced reporting, and priority email support.

What's included in Enterprise?
Enterprise includes everything in Growth, plus SSO/SAML login, a dedicated account manager, custom onboarding, and a 99.9% uptime SLA.

Is there a free trial?
Yes. All plans include a 14-day free trial with full feature access — no credit card required to start.

Can I switch plans later?
Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade at any time from Workspace Settings > Billing. Downgrades take effect at the start of your next billing cycle; upgrades are prorated immediately.

What happens if I exceed my member limit on Starter?
You'll be prompted to upgrade to Growth. Existing members are never removed automatically — we just pause new invites until you upgrade or remove inactive members.

Do you offer discounts for nonprofits or students?
Yes, we offer a 30% discount for verified nonprofits and educational institutions. Contact support with proof of status to apply.
