Nimbus Stack — Account & Billing FAQ

How do I reset my password?
Go to the login page and click "Forgot Password." A reset link is sent to your registered email and expires after 1 hour. If you don't receive it, check your spam folder or contact support.

How do I change my email address?
Go to Account Settings > Profile > Email. You'll need to confirm the change via a verification link sent to the new address. Your old email remains active until the new one is confirmed.

How is billing calculated for teams that add or remove members mid-month?
Billing is prorated daily. If you add a member on day 10 of a 30-day cycle, you're charged for the remaining 20 days at that member's rate. Removing a member issues a prorated credit toward your next invoice.

Can I have multiple workspaces under one account?
Yes. Each workspace is billed separately, but you can switch between them from the workspace picker in the top-left corner without needing to log in again.

What payment methods are accepted?
We accept all major credit cards and, for Enterprise plans, ACH bank transfer and invoicing with net-30 terms.

How do I cancel my subscription?
Go to Workspace Settings > Billing > Cancel Plan. Your workspace remains active in read-only mode for 30 days after cancellation, after which data is permanently deleted unless you export it first.

Who can access billing settings?
Only workspace Admins can view or modify billing information. Editors and Viewers do not have access to this section.
